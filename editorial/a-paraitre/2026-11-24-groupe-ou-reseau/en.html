Generator or grid: how to choose the power source?

24 November 20263 min read

Key points

  • The grid first: look at what the venue can supply, then at what a temporary connection can add.
  • With Enedis, the French distribution network operator, a temporary connection is low voltage up to 250 kVA, with a threshold at 36 kVA. Above that, it moves to high voltage.
  • When the grid is not enough: generator, battery pack, or both in a hybrid setup, sized from the power assessment.

Before ordering a generator, one question comes first: can the grid supply the power? This is our rule: we study the feasibility of a connection to the existing grid before using generators as the main source.

1. What the venue can already supply

A site connected to the grid has a import capacity: the maximum power it can draw, set by its contract. It tells you what the venue can supply without reinforcement. If the event's power assessment fits within it, the source is found.

2. What a temporary connection can add

In France, Enedis provides temporary connections for installations that are not permanent: construction sites, funfairs, fêtes, markets. They are short-term up to 28 days, long-term beyond.

PowerConnection
Up to 36 kVALow voltage, three-phase above 12 kVA
From 36 to 250 kVALow voltage
Above 250 kVAHigh voltage (HTA), with no short-term offer

In three-phase 400 V, 36 kVA means 52 A per phase, the rating of a P17 63 A socket. 250 kVA means 361 A per phase, a Powerlock 400 A connection.

The request goes through an electricity supplier, which Enedis advises contacting 25 days before the power is needed. After the delivery point, the equipment is supplied and installed by the applicant: this is where the event's distribution starts.

3. When the grid is not enough

Remote site, insufficient power, limited or costly connection: power must then be produced on site. Three solutions, alone or combined:

  • a generator, sized to the actual need: too big for its load, it clogs up;
  • a battery pack, which powers the event without a combustion engine;
  • both in a hybrid setup: the battery supplies low loads and covers the peaks, and the generator only starts when demand or recharging requires it.

Grid and generator can also be combined.

The grid first, the generator second, and never bigger than the need.

Studying the connection before bringing out the generators is part of our responsible energy approach.

To know whether the grid is enough, you first need the power to plan for: that is the role of the power assessment.
