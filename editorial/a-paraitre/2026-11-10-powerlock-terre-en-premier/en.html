Powerlock: why connect the earth first?

10 November 20263 min read

Key points

  • A three-phase Powerlock connection means five single-pole cables: earth, neutral and the three phases.
  • Connect in the order earth, neutral, then L1, L2 and L3, and disconnect in reverse order: the earth is the first connected and the last disconnected.
  • Some boxes enforce this order mechanically; these connectors remain reserved for trained professionals.

Powerlock connectors are used for high currents, in 400 A and 660 A versions, up to 1,000 V AC. They are single-pole: one connector per conductor. A three-phase connection therefore needs five separate cables, four without neutral. Touch-proof contacts, colour coding by conductor, keying and locking: a locked connector can only be removed with a tool.

Since each conductor is connected separately, the connection order matters.

Earth first

Picture a piece of equipment with an insulation fault: a phase touches its metal frame. If that phase is connected before the earth, the frame becomes live with no protective conductor to carry the fault away. With the earth connected first, the protection is in place before the first live conductor arrives.

On disconnection, it is the other way round: the phases first, the earth last. Manufacturers call it "first mate, last break": the earth is the first connected and the last disconnected.

Neutral before the phases

Without the neutral, single-phase equipment connected to different phases ends up in series between two phases, at 400 V. The voltage across each item then depends on their respective loads: some receive far more than their 230 V and can be destroyed. The neutral is therefore connected before the phases and disconnected after them.

Boxes that enforce the order

So-called sequential Powerlock boxes make the mistake impossible: on one of them, a key opens access to the earth, the neutral port only opens once the earth is locked, then come L1, L2 and L3. The connector colours, which follow those of the conductors, also help avoid picking the wrong cable.

These connectors are not handled like a household plug: they are reserved for trained professionals.

Earth first, earth last: the protection is in place before the first live conductor, and stays until the last.
