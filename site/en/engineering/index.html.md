## From site visit to calculation notes.

Every deliverable carries a full title block (title, author, revision index, status) and a revision history. Bilingual French and English deliverables on request.

### Site visit

Analysis of the existing installation, infrastructure, access and technical constraints, with a written report.

### Grid connection study

Assessment of the existing network, import capacity, temporary HV or LV connection procedure with the network operator.

### Power assessment

Inventory of each trade's requirements, criticality levels (main, backup, UPS) and realistic diversity factors.

### Single-line diagram

Overall HV and LV architecture for each site, annotated and indexed, from the delivery point down to the distribution boards.

### Site layout plans

Positions of sources, transformers, distribution cabinets and boxes, in line with safety distances and access requirements.

### Cable route plans

Routing of power networks, crossing areas and cable protectors, coordinated with the other technical packages.

### Calculation notes

Equipment selection, cable sizes, protection selectivity, voltage drop.

### Financial analysis

Checking the cost of equipment, cabling and installation, so the budget is planned in advance and kept to.

## No more, no less than what is needed.

- **Realistic diversity factors.** Our coefficients are based on our operational experience, not only on manufacturers' data sheets.
- **Sources as close as possible to the loads.** Shorter cable runs, less voltage drop, less equipment deployed.
- **Existing installations first.** Using what is already in place whenever possible is more sustainable and more economical.
