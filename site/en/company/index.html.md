# People who made event power distribution their profession.

> Dark Side Energy, an SARL based in Carvin, France: people who have made event power distribution their profession since 2016. Join us.

Source: https://www.darkside-energy.com/en/company/ · Dark Side Energy · Updated: 2026-10-06

Since 2016, our aim has been to support you on the electrical side of your projects, from the initial idea through to delivery: supply, distribution, engineering, technical management and coordination.

## Here comes Dark Side Energy!

A team of electrical technicians, project managers and engineers specialising in events, in France and abroad.

Our way of working grew out of a simple principle: a design only has value if the teams who will implement it on site can actually use it. Engineering, technical and production management, hands-on technical operations: the many trades within our team let us anticipate constraints and deliver installations that are true to the reality on the ground.

And because electricity remains a matter of safety, our teams receive continuous training: electrical authorisations (French *habilitations*) kept up to date, CAD drawing, mentoring of junior staff.
