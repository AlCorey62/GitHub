Who is Dark Side Energy?

Dark Side Energy is a French event power distribution company, founded in 2016 and based in Carvin, in the Pas-de-Calais (northern France). Its electrical technicians, project managers and engineers work in France and internationally across power distribution, technical management, general coordination, engineering office and consulting, for trade shows, concerts, sporting events, festivals and ceremonies. Our credo

Field experience in the service of engineering.

A study is only of value if the teams who will implement it on site can use it. Our way of working was born from this principle.

The many disciplines within our team, such as engineering, technical and production management or operational technical work, allow us to anticipate constraints and produce deliverables that reflect the reality on site.

And from the grid connection agreement to the client's socket, the whole chain is covered in-house: no grey area between high and low voltage.

Responsible energy

Moving beyond the “all-generator” approach.

We master alternative technologies and promote them to all our clients, to move towards less energy-intensive solutions.

  • Grid first. The feasibility of a connection to the existing grid is assessed before any use of generators.
  • Hybrid solutions. HVO or Stage V generators, battery packs, battery and generator hybrids, grid and generator combinations.
  • Right-sizing. Power assessments calibrated on our field data: no systematic overcapacity.
