Our beginnings

  1. May 2016

    Dark Side Energy is founded

    People who made event power distribution their profession.

  2. May 2016

    Brest International Maritime Festival

    First assignment, entrusted to us by Revolt Location: many generators, temporary metered grid connections, around twenty self-powered lighting towers and more than 400 distribution boxes to deploy along the quaysides of the port of Brest.

  3. October 2016

    Lille Grand Palais

    Two trade shows entrusted to us since September for power distribution, ADSL and telephony: Créer, then Sénioreva.

  4. 2019 and 2020

    Dark Side Energy SARL

    Company registered in November 2019, then head office established in Carvin (Pas-de-Calais) in November 2020.

  5. Today

    From Zéniths to stadiums

    Concerts, sports competitions, trade shows and ceremonies, in France and abroad.
