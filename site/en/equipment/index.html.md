## Modulo 5-channel protectors, wheelchair-accessible ramps.

Designed for events and for buildings open to the public (French *ERP*), Modulo cable protectors adapt to any type of event. Sturdy and practical, they are ideal for temporary installations.

- **Channels**: 5 channels per section (also available with 3 channels)
- **Capacity per channel**: One 5G35 cable or one single-core cable, 240 mm² maximum
- **Modularity**: Sections add up in multiples of 5 channels, with no limit
- **Ramps**: Compliant with accessibility standards for people with reduced mobility: safe, easy access for everyone
- **Use**: Events, buildings open to the public, temporary installations
- **Service**: Turnkey hire, laid and removed by our teams

## Generators, batteries and grid connections.

The grid when possible, recent generators or batteries when necessary, and often a bit of both. Our engineering office guides you towards the solution best suited to your power assessment.

[Our responsible energy approach](https://www.darkside-energy.com/en/responsible-energy/)

- **Generators**: Stage V in twin versions: 30, 60, 100, 200, 300 and 600 kVA. Also 320 kVA Stage III.
- **Battery packs**: 45 kVA / 55 kWh, 90 kVA / 120 kWh, 300 kVA / 420 kWh
- **Fuel**: 3,000 L tanks, including an HVO tank with 750 L of AdBlue
- **Grid connections**: Temporary connections on *tarif bleu* (French grid connection up to 36 kVA) and *tarif jaune* (36 to 250 kVA), up to 400 A
- **Also**: 100 kW direct-expansion air conditioning, LED lighting
