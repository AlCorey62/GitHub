Our services

What is event power distribution?

Event power distribution means starting from a venue's main power supply, or supplies, and distributing electricity to the places requested by the organisers, in compliance with safety standards: cable sizes suited to the voltage, current and length; protection of people; protection of power circuits. It relies on distribution cabinets and distribution boxes.

Why must each stand at a trade show have its own distribution box?

Each stand must have its own box fitted with a 30 mA RCD, which protects people. Powering a stand from the neighbouring stand's box, known in France as ilotage, is systematically refused by safety officers: in the event of a fault, the neighbouring exhibitor could reset the RCD without knowing that someone is still in contact with the faulty appliance.

What is the difference between technical management and general coordination?

Technical management takes charge of the technical organisation of an event: recruiting qualified staff, managing subcontractors, logistics and coordinating service providers. General coordination means joining the organiser's teams in a key role: technical director, production manager, production assistant or coordinator.

What does the engineering office produce?

Site visits, grid connection studies, power assessments, single-line diagrams, site layout and cable routing plans, calculation notes (cable sizes, selectivity, voltage drops) and financial analyses. Deliverables can be supplied in French and in English.

Equipment and energy

Can I hire equipment without a technician?

No. Equipment is available for turnkey hire only: distribution cabinets and boxes, P17 and Powerlock cables, Modulo cable protectors with wheelchair-accessible ramps, generators and battery packs are supplied with Dark Side Energy's teams, who install and operate them. Dark Side Energy offers neither dry hire nor sales. See the Equipment page.

What is a Twin zero-interruption generator set?

It consists of two synchronised generators, each supplying 50% of the load. If one fails, the other takes over the entire load without any interruption, which protects computers and sound and lighting consoles. Some configurations synchronise 3 or 4 generators.

What are the alternatives to diesel generators?

Connection to the existing grid where possible, which is studied first; battery packs (from 45 kVA / 55 kWh to 300 kVA / 420 kWh); Stage V generators or generators running on HVO; and hybrid battery and generator systems. A power assessment that sizes the installation to actual needs also reduces consumption. See our responsible energy approach.

Technical

How much power can you draw from a P17 socket?

Theoretical maximum apparent power, with no diversity factor and no margin: 3.7 kVA for a 16 A single-phase socket at 230 V, 11.1 kVA for a 16 A three-phase P17, 22.2 kVA for a 32 A three-phase P17, 43.6 kVA for a 63 A P17, 86.6 kVA for a 125 A P17 and 277.1 kVA for a 400 A Powerlock, at 400 V. All the reference figures are in the electrical calculator.

How do you convert kW to amps?

Single-phase at 230 V: I = P ÷ (230 × cos φ). Three-phase at 400 V: I = P ÷ (√3 × 400 × cos φ). For example, 22 kW in three-phase with a cos φ of 0.9 gives about 35.3 A per phase.

What is the maximum permissible voltage drop?

For an installation supplied directly from the public low-voltage grid, the French standard NF C 15-100 sets 3% for lighting and 5% for other uses. For an installation supplied from a private delivery or transformer substation, these values rise to 6% and 8%. Voltage drop increases with cable length and current: calculate it here.

Why does a distribution box “trip”?

Either an appliance is faulty and leaking current: above 30 mA, the RCD cuts the power to protect people. Or too many appliances are plugged into the same circuit: the circuit breaker cuts the power to protect the installation. In both cases, the box has done its job.

What is the diversity factor?

It is a coefficient between 0 and 1 that reflects the fact that not all appliances run at full power at the same time. Applied in the power assessment, it allows the installation to be sized to actual needs, without overcapacity. Try it with our power assessment tool.
